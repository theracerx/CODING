Did you know?

May 24 is International Pansexual and Panromantic Visibility Day. 

Download

Celebrated on May 24th every year, this day uplifts, celebrates, and promotes awareness of pansexual and panromantic folks.

“Pan” is Greek for “all,” and pansexuality and panromanticism generally refer to attraction to all genders, or attraction regardless of gender, though individuals will have their own interpretation of their identity.

If you are pansexual and/or panromantic, have a very happy International Pansexual and Panromantic Visibility Day celebrating your awesome self! Let us know who you’ll be celebrating!

A discussion group organized by Autism Edmonton that meets monthly on every second Wednesday. Learn more here. 

 

Proudly Outspoken Toastmasters

Free demo meetings until enough people join to create a chartered club. They meet monthly on the 1st and 3rd Sunday. Learn more on Facebook or LinkedIn.

 

Team Edmonton 2SLGBTQ+ Friendly Sports & Recreation 

Find out about opportunities to participate in low cost 2SLGBTQ+ friendly sports and recreation in Edmonton. Current winter drop-in activities include yoga, pickleball, badminton, volleyball, swimming and water polo. Learn more at teamedmonton.ca

 

Edmonton Lesbian Event Network (ELEN)

ELEN is a 2SLGBTQQIA+ inclusive and volunteer-lead social club that hosts a variety of events and activities throughout Edmonton. Find out more at facebook.com/ELENyeg.

 

Pe Metawe Games

Pe Metawe Games is an Indigenous-owned table top game and role playing game store located on Treaty 6 territory in the city of Edmonton. They host low cost 2SLGBTQQIA+ friendly events each month. Find out more at pemetawe.com

 

Rainbow Roots (By Centre for Sexuality in Calgary)

A drop-in discussion group for parents of sexually and gender diverse children aged 12 and up. Reserve a spot

 

PFlag Canada

Another drop-in support group with chapters across the province for members of the community, allies, and parents. Find out more at: https://pflagcanada.ca/

 

Skipping Stone

An amazing Calgary based organization helping queer folx across the province. They have weekly in person and online events and support groups for youth, trans, non-binary, genderqueer, BIPOC, older trans. As well as support groups for parents, caregivers, and allies. Learn more at: https://www.skippingstone.ca/groups

Transgender / Non-Binary Community Resource

A virtual safe space specifically for APS Employees who are Transgender or Non-Binary, or have Transgender and Non-Binary Family Members.

The goal of the Alberta Family Service (AFS) is sharing encouragement, supporting one another, asking questions and sharing helpful resources for both groups. In the true spirit of inclusion, family in this safe space includes biological, adoptive and chosen family.

Please note: This space is specifically by and for people who are APS employees, to be accessed on personal time. Though promoted by the ERG, this is not an official program of the LGBTQ2S+ ERG.

To get more information or become part of the AFS virtual safe space, please reach out to AlbertaFamilyService@gmail.com. Your initial contact should be via a GoA email address in order to confirm that you work for the GoA. The service is also looking for two moderators to encourage conversation and sharing on the site.

Learning Opportunities

The following learning opportunities are available through the APS Professional Development Framework. 

Access these course through My Learning in 1GX. Please log into 1GX then click on the “Profile” tile, then click on the “Employee Files” drop down near the top left of the screen, then choose “Learning”. Search for the course using the Find Learning box. Further instructions can be found on Hello 1GX. 

Mosaic: Gender and Sexuality in the Workplace is a half-day course that is an introductory awareness and ally training session that offers a safe space to explore concepts of sexuality and gender, supporting freedom of self in the APS workplace. (search word: Mosaic)

Foundations of Unconscious Bias is an e-course that builds awareness about unconscious bias (UB) by introducing foundational UB concepts and definitions, the cognitive science behind UB, and common types of UB. (search word: bias)

The Disability Awareness in the Workplace course supports people leaders and Human Resources (HR) to establish safer and inclusive work environments for employees with disabilities. (search word: disability)

Foundations for Supervisors Training – While some content of these free E-courses is specific to working in the APS, a lot of it is useful if involved in leadership in ERGs or in the community. (search word: supervisor)
